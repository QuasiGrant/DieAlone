## The 30 m line rule: replace
A clear line from somewhere on a trail within 30 m proves visibility, not finding. A player finds what enters the forward view while walking. Proposed rule (Grant decides):
- From a trail point within 30 m, eye 1.6 m, a clear line to the place's top and centre, **by renderers** (pixel or ray against meshes, not colliders);
- the place within 45 degrees of the direction of travel, in at least one direction along that trail;
- it subtends 1 degree or more vertically (about 30 px of 1976);
- one labelled frame per place from that point in the sheet, so a reviewer can confirm by eye.
Places inside the camp clearing may instead be found from the cabin door or the camp sign by the same test.
