4. **The trails work, with C6.** Both carry a far ring (Quill 14). C6 takes the T leg out from under the stair (Gate_817: Ramp1's rail on its line) and opens the ramp-foot bay to the east, so the blaze shows from the leg's first frame.
5. **The deck sees the stack top** (lamp 64 of 64 eyes on paper; section 6), not the table, booth or boulder field (Main3_BuildNotes), so Quill 10's paper goes on the top rail and the talus (C7).
6. **Water at the barrel: keep `Take water`, some days dry (about half, rolled at dawn; playtest).** It never beats the pump: from the door the barrel is 299 m by the pump or 324 m the other way, against the pump's 120, and the east ring passes the pump first. 7. **His hours (my call; Quill writes what happens):** by day on top in his chair; on hand days at the table from dawn to dusk, the top chair empty, lamp lit; at night in his tent, lamp lit, never at the table. The phone rings only by day. Which rings, and who answers when he is on top: Quill (private).
