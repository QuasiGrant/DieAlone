## 2. Living: where everything is (real practice)
1. **Size: 6 x 4.5 m inside, ceiling 2.7, is right.** A USFS L-4 lookout cab, 14 x 14 ft (4.3 m square), held a keeper's whole life; a ground guard cabin of about 14 x 20 ft (unverified) is our 27 m2. Bigger reads as a lodge; smaller fails the 1 m path. Keep the shell; move the inside.

## 4. Walkability rules
1. One clear path 1 m wide from the door to the bunk front, the stove front, the desk chair and the shelf (drawn). The middle floor X 1.3 to 5.4, Z 0.5 to 3.3 holds nothing.
2. Every gap is under 0.6 m (the capsule cannot enter) or 1.0 m and over. No pocket in between.
3. Thresholds flush; rugs and wall-mounted things have no collider; the chair is static, not a Rigidbody.
4. The player spawns standing on the floor at the bunk edge, never on the mattress.
5. The door leaf never pushes the player (Door.cs behaviour unverified; Rook checks).
