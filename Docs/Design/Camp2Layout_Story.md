Camp 2, the Lover. What it says: a man who climbed as high as he could to watch for someone's car. A camp set for two with one person in it. He waits sober; there is no drink here.
1. L. Payphone on its post at the stack foot, on the side the trails arrive from, clear of the ramp foot (298.9, 107.8). Its own small hood light. A wire from it toward the phone pole, so the two read as one line.
2. L. Card table on an upturned cooler lid beside the payphone, the table edge within cord reach of the handset (about 1 m). Valley M5 has post and table 3.2 m apart; too far.
3. L. Three places at the table: his seat facing the phone, the player's seat, and a third place nobody takes. The player never sits in the third place.
4. D. At the third place: a clean tin mug upside down and a hand dealt face down, squared, never picked up. His side: his hand, a lantern, matches for a tally.
5. L. From the player's seat, with him standing at the phone with his back turned: his camp in view around the table (his kit, the barrel, the ramp up).
6. L. Rain barrel at the stack foot, reached from the table area. D: room for it to lie on its side.
7. L. Stack top: tent with a zip door, cold white lamp, his chair at the east edge facing the road. Room beside the chair for the player to stand and talk to him. Lamp and the edge both read from the deck.
8. L. From the chair, the highway and the gate T in view over the trees (Valley 7.7). Unverified: one frame from the chair proves it.
9. D. On top: two mugs, one sleeping bag unrolled, letters under stones by the tent.
10. L. Boulder field: three spots among the rocks where loose paper can lodge, reachable on foot, paper readable from the deck.
11. D. Default state: lamp steady, tent open, handset on its hook, cards face up or in his hand. Nothing zipped, dark or off the hook by default.
