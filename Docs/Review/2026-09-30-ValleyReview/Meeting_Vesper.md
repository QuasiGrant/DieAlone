## 1. Grant's look notes
- **Forest looks bad, too few trees: agree.** The top view shows about ten small groves and one edge row on a bare tan floor. Style 5.8 asks for giant groves with gaps; we built a few islands. The tower-sightline rule shaped the planting, and it should not come first. I agree that we overweighted it.
- **Lighting odd and bright: agree.** Sun at 32 degrees from bearing 200 is midday light, not late afternoon. The ridges are flat, untextured planes lit evenly to the same tan as the floor. The sky is flat grey-blue with a white smear. The haze is lifted everywhere. No sunset, no value structure (Style 6.1.3), so it reads as a washed-out test level.
- **Paths look like grass: agree.** The path and the ground are the same colour and texture. Stops are invisible walls with no rock, log or fence to show them. That is a look failure as much as a usability one.
- **North feels empty: agree.** The top view shows the north third as open dirt with scattered specks and one grove. There is no landmark, no grove wall and nothing leading the eye.
- **Store tiny: agree it reads wrong.** From outside it reads as a shed. Whatever the interior becomes (Wren and Rook to answer whether it is its own scene), the outside must read as a store at 20 m: sign, porch, lot light.

## 2. Why I passed what Grant rejects
- I compared each capture with the previous one ("brighter than 8.9g, PASS"), not against an absolute bar or a reference board. Every pass was relative.
- I proposed the 32-degree sun myself (Edges.md) to light Camp 3. Sun, ambient, crushBlacks and darkCorners all went up in one step. That is the overcorrection, and it is mine.
- I judged a few still frames, picked by the builder and taken at 3840 wide. In play, at 540 rows and moving, the picture has no dark anchor and the flat ridge planes fill half the view.
- I judged exposure only and waved away the gray geometry around it ("fine for gray"). Grant sees the whole frame, not the exposure.

## 3. Minimum look before Grant walks any map
- **Trees:** grove density along every trail edge and each ridge foot. No open ground wider than 30 m unless it is a designed clearing. The north gets a grove wall and one landmark.
- **Paths:** a separate packed-dirt material, clearly lighter or darker than the grass, with stones or logs along the edges. Every stop sits behind a visible object; no bare invisible walls.
- **Terrain:** a textured rock or scree material on the ridges. No flat untextured planes in view of any trail.
- **Lighting:** agreed values with a low raking sun that reads as late afternoon (I propose trying 18 to 22 degrees, one change at a time). The frame must hold a real dark (the foreground under trees) and a real bright (the sky at the horizon).

## 4. How I change my checks
- Walk it myself in Play mode, or review a route capture every 25 m along each trail at game resolution, plus a top-down view. No builder-picked frames only.
- Judge against an absolute bar written into Style.md (the list above, plus a reference board), never against the last capture.
- Lighting changes go in one variable per retake, with before and after shown side by side.
- Fail anything I would not show Grant, gray or not. "Fine for gray" is no longer a pass reason.
