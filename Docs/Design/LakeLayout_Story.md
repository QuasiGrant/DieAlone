# Lake: what it must hold (story)
Quill, 2026-10-02. **Draft** for 8.23; Sable places, Grant decides. L = layout, needs a place now. D = dressing, Milestone 11. Positions: Valley.md 6 (M7, M8, E6). Unverified sightlines are for Rook's frames.

Boathouse, Tuesday. What it says: a boathouse with no boat and nobody in it, kept by a cat. Someone put out a bowl with her name on it. One chair, turned to the water. The one place on the map you breathe out.
1. L. Her step on the north side, over the water, facing the tower: a landing with room for the bowl, the chair and the player beside her. Seen from the deck (hard).
2. L. Bowl on the step, reached standing, not leaning over water. D: her name on it in pencil capitals.
3. L. Chair on the step beside the bowl, where you eat your own fish. LookBoards puts a chair on the pump dock; the story wants it here. One chair at the lake.
4. L. Roof she can sit on, seen from the deck, low enough at one stilt that a propped oar reaches it.
5. L. Under the deck: stilts with a gap at the water line, a space she can be shut in and the player can wade to.
6. L. Inside: one room, an empty boat slip, a shelf or hook to set a found lamp on. Door and gangway on the east wall as built. D: nets, oars, crates, cold stove pipe.
7. D. Window lamp: LookBoards lights it from day two. Nobody lives there. Who lights it is open (Grant).
Fishing
8. L. Two rod rests within 10 m of the step: reeds on the west side (low, green-brown, shallow) and the stilts' end (dark deep water, no reeds). Each faces open water with far shore and sky in frame. They must look different from any distance.
Pump and dock, the Water chore
9. L. Plank steps with a rail down the bank to the dock; the pump on the dock end; a bucket at it. No path that just stops. Pump handle and dock surface seen from the deck.
10. L. The pump's intake pipe into the water off the dock end, where the player can kneel and hold it.
11. L. A strip of shore by the dock where a darker band at the water's edge can be looked at.
Shore and the wade limit
12. L. Shore path, pump to boathouse, north bank. The overturned rowboat at about 43 m, with an oar that can be picked up and carried.
13. L. Past the boathouse the path heads east toward the road, traffic faintly heard on it. A stretch east of the step in a deck frame.
14. L. Three events send the player into the water: under the stilts, off the step, where something drifts ashore. Valley.md 8 sets the wade limit at the visible edge. They need a knee-deep band with a visible stop (reeds, stakes, a drop to black). Sable's call.
15. L. Soft ground near the step, enough for a small grave.
Night: off the Ward route; from camp only the boathouse window. One dawn event, none at night. Why each: Docs/Private/LakeLayout_Quill.md (private).
