Grant's "why is this taking so long", "how long", "ETA?" are curiosity, not pressure. Answer plainly and keep the plan as is.

**Why:** 2026-09-30 and 2026-10-01, twice in a row I answered a timing question by offering a faster path; he replied "Keep going, Im just asking" and "I dont need faster, I was just asking".

**How to apply:** Give the honest estimate and the reasons. Don't propose shortcuts or reorder work unless he asks for speed. Related: [[feedback-play-before-grant]].
