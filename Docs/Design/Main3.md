# Main3 paper brief

**DRAFT, revision 3, 2026-09-28, Sable. Nothing here is decided.** Rewritten for the 2026-09-28 loop lines; the 200 x 200 m seven-place brief is gone. The loop lives in Docs/Design/DailyLoop.md and is not repeated here. Map: Docs/Design/Main3_map.svg. Numbers provisional until the Milestone 7 simulator.

Coordinates in metres, origin south-west, x east, z north. Heights absolute (metres above map base 0). Walk times at 2.5 m/s.

## 1. Purpose

1. A map where every walk is a cost the day has to pay, and no two places cost or feel the same.
2. The tower sees every place it must check; the two places that take from you, the Ward and the cave, are the two it cannot see.

## 2. Shape

1. **Size: 400 x 300 m.** Fire to the west, the man-made front zone a thin strip on the east edge, forest between.
2. West edge: cliff along x 10, valley floor -40 m below, burning ridge 300 to 500 m beyond.
3. North-west: the spur, high ground to 36 m, with the Ward on its cliff edge behind the Tor.
4. South-west: a ravine with the cultist cave, below a rock rim.
5. Centre: the keeper's camp on a knoll with the tower, the lake to the south, Camp 3 sunk in a hollow to the west.
6. East: Camp 1 and Camp 2 in the forest, then the fence and the front zone.
7. Giant trees: emergents 40 to 50 m tall, trunks 6 to 10 m. Tops never above 50 m absolute (the spur excepted). On the knoll, at most 42 m tall. Mid canopy 18 m.

### 3.1 Front zone
1. A strip 52 m wide along the east edge (x 348 to 400), the only man-made ground: straight road, fence, parking lot, office, store.
2. Road: straight north-south along x 376, full map length, barrier at both ends. The only straight line on the map.
3. Fence: chain-link along x 348, one gate at (348, 168).
4. Parking lot 30 x 40 m centred (376, 168). Office (382, 206). Store (380, 132): small, unstaffed, a lit cooler sign.
5. Sounds: machine sounds only (generator, cooler hum, light buzz, a car ticking as it cools). Hollis.

## 6. Break the uniformity (what changed)

1. Sizes: 60 m (Camp 1), 36 m (camp), 25 m sunken (Camp 3), 12 m rock top (Camp 2), a 110 m lake, a lot and a road.
2. Distances from camp: 80 m (pump) to 307 m (office). Near places are cheap; far ones eat the day.
3. Heights: -6 m (cave), -4 m (hollow), 8 m (knoll), 16 m (rock top), 36 m (Ward).
4. Light: warm yellow, cold white, green, sodium orange, and one cold place.
5. Actions: each camp gives a different need (Food, Water, Warmth). The office has the store. The lake has the sure water.
