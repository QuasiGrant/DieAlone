## Proposed night rule: night paths (for Pim, Wren, Grant)

Night grey on all 14 trails is 2 to 22, trail and floor alike, so texture cannot pass. At night the only errand is the Ward (DECISIONS 2026-09-28), so only the camp clearing, Camp to J and J to Ward are walked. Proposal:

1. **Scope.** The night path rule applies only to the night route: the camp clearing, Camp to J, J to Ward. Other trails are judged at night only on hard line 4 (crests, practicals).
2. **The keeper's light is the path light.** A carried kerosene lamp, #FFA860, soft 6 to 8 m radius, no beam. No flashlight cone: it reads modern and wrong for the tape. This is a mechanic, so Grant and Sable decide it; whether one exists is unverified (Farm Tools KeroseneLamp is a prop only).
3. **Pim's grey at night is measured inside the lamp.** 20 grey or more at 5 m only. The 20 m point is replaced by: every night frame on the route shows the next marker.
4. **Markers, not texture, carry 20 m and beyond.** Pale granite edge stones on the outer edge of each bend, every 3 to 5 m, top faces at most #D8CCB4, so they outline the trail in the lamp. Painted tin blazes on trunks at eye height every 15 to 25 m, lit only by the lamp, with no emission (Style 4.6). The J cairn lamp and the chute-foot lantern are destination lights you can see from the one before (fog end 60 m).
5. **Sky slot.** Keep the canopy open over the trail so the #0C1016 sky and the west fire glow show as a pale strip between black crowns. Wherever trees stand, this is the trail you read from far off.
6. **No moon.** 6.2.1 stays. Moonlight lifts trail and floor together, so it gains no contrast. It also adds a second large light against the fire and spoils the reveal.
7. **No reflectors on trails.** The road's white reflector posts stay on the road. On a footpath they read as a park, not a lookout.
