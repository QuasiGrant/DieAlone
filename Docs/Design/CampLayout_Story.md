# Keeper's camp: what it must hold (story)

Quill, 2026-10-02. **Draft** for 8.21; Sable places, Grant decides. Serves DECISIONS 2026-10-02 (a real home). L = layout, needs a place now. D = dressing, Milestone 11.

What it says: a man on a government post. Issued kit, one of each, kept in order out of habit. Nothing on the walls about who he was. The forms are the only neat thing. The bunk is never made.

Cabin
1. L. Bunk, north wall. From the pillow, one look takes in the door, the stove and kettle, and the desk. The morning starts on that frame.
2. L. Stove with room to crouch and feed it; firebox door faces the room (things get burned in it). D: kettle on top.
3. L. Desk under the west window, one chair, the tower in the window when seated. Report box on it. A desk light that shows in the window from outside at night.
4. L. Eating spot: a shelf-table by the stove, or the desk. D: one plate, one mug, one pot on a hook.
5. L. Supply shelf: tins, water jug, matches. Nothing counted (DECISIONS 2026-09-27). D: issued stencils.
6. L. Washstand with basin and towel by the door. No mirror.
7. L. Lamp hook inside the door. The lamp hangs there by day, gone at night: the night walk starts at this hook. Draft; the lamp's day place is not decided.
8. L. Door swings inward, full swing clear, seen from the bunk. It must be able to stand open and to slam.
9. L. Clear floor from door to bunk (the 1.5 m aisle). D: boots by the bunk, clipboard on a nail, issued blanket.

Camp
10. L. Porch or wide step at the door: room to set a box down; ash on it must show prints.
11. L. Woodpile and chopping block a few steps from the door (Warmth). Loose planks in the pile.
12. L. Outhouse at the clearing edge on a short worn path, door facing away from the cabin.
13. L. Fire pit with one flat rock at its edge, big enough for a plate.
14. L. Tower foot in clear sight of the cabin door; the stair visible up through the legs from the foot; a floor hatch into the cab. Fixed, countable step count.
15. Generator behind the cabin (built). D: fuel can, a cable to the cabin.
