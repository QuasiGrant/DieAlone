Grant reads drawings better than numbers. Map and space proposals go to him as an SVG (plan plus a side view where height matters), in the style of Docs/Design/Main3_map.svg; the .md carries the numbers for Rook and Marlow.

**Why:** stated by Wren for the valley draft, 2026-09-29; Grant approved the valley build "from your drawing".
**How to apply:** any design that changes the map, sightlines or heights gets a drawing with sightline arrows and a cross-section inset. Check every sightline claim in all bearings, not just the main one (Marlow caught due-west-only checks on the valley draft); see [[valley-lessons]].
