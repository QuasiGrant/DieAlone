   **Night-2 check, the climb stays silent (DN 7.5): PASS on rev 10.** Every eye on the climb is 18 m or more below the crest on the line west: chute and shelf (ground 12 to 41) 39 m or more; cwm (P3 eye 53.6, rims 76 to 80) 22 m; leg 4 (Valley 4.5) 19 m or more; P4 (eye 61.6) 18.4 m. Close under a tall wall is where its sound shadow is deepest, so the climb being quieter than J and the camp is right, not a cheat. Vesper hears it on the build.
