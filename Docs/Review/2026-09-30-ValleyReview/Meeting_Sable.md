## 2. The sightline over-index
Valley.md rev 7 is honest evidence: seven revisions, nearly all margins. A cap rock was added only to block one ray. There is no line about what the player does, sees or feels on any leg, how dense the forest is, or how a path reads. I optimized what could be measured, and Marlow's checks rewarded it. What the map should optimize for, in order:
1. The loop plays: every trip has a reason (a need, a resident, an event) and the trip itself is worth taking.
2. Readability: paths read as paths, stops read as stops, the next place is visible at each junction.
3. The fantasy: a forest of giants, dense and dark; the Ward reveal at night.
4. Pacing and variety: no two legs alike, nightly walks short enough to repeat.
5. Hard constraints, checked once each, not tuned: Ward and fire hidden on day 1, cave hidden. "The tower sees every place" is a loose check, not a margin table.

## 3. Valley shape
A horseshoe open to the east. The W ridge stays the high dark wall with the fire and the Ward knob behind it. The N and S arms hook round the fire at their west ends (they carry the hiding) and fall off eastward into forested foothills that end before the fence. No E ridge. Past the fence the ground is low and open: the highway runs north-south in plain view of the lot and office, the gate drive meets it at a T, and far ranges sit beyond. The player can see the ordinary world on every trip to the office and can never reach it (Firewatch's distant town, Exit 8's corridor you cannot leave). The fire stays hidden because it is west; opening the east costs nothing there. Paper only; Grant confirms.

## 4. The climb
Walked every night, so it must change and stay short (target under 2 min). Legs of different length and grade: one short steep chute through boulders, one exposed ledge with a drop, one through burned snags, one long easy traverse. One landmark per leg. The climb changes as WARD falls: a stone moved, a rune post missing, an offering left (Exit 8: spot the difference). Events keep the platforms.

## 5. The north
A north loop trail from Camp 1 west to J along the N ridge foot, so the north is walked, not only seen. On it: the densest giant grove on the map, forage patch C (Food on some days), the creek spring, the payphone (Disco Elysium homage), and one ruin events can use. Candidate home for the Artist's escape-room puzzle, whose place is open.

## 6. How I change
1. Every map doc opens with a player section: per leg, what the player does, sees and feels. Numbers follow.
2. No revision whose only change is sightline margins. If a fix needs new geometry for one ray, I ask whether it matters first.
3. I sign off a build only after reading player-eye screenshots every 20 m on each route (I read images; I do not drive the Editor), plus a list: paths readable, every stop visible and tested, every warp lands on a path, dev menu features findable.
4. Grant sees a walkable rough version before paper refinement, not after.
