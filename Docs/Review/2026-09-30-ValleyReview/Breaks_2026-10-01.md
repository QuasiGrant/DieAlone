# Main3 breaks hunt: falls, traps, walk-through props

Marlow, 2026-10-01. For Grant's look-around ("several warps have you fall through the map").

## Method

- Scene: Main3 at commit 9a8bcfc, which includes Rook's warp reseat c42893a. The flood, traps, clip and warp-radius checks ran on it.
- The frames and the door test ran at 16:58 on Rook's uncommitted cave edits.
- Mover: PlayerController.Step with dt 0.02. Walk means no sprint and no jump. Sprint-jump means sprint with jump held.
- Flood:
  - Every place reachable from all 26 warps, on a 2 m grid keyed by height.
  - 8 headings x walk and sprint-jump.
  - 27,165 standing places, about 540,000 moves.
  - A reverse search then found the places with no way back to any warp.
- Each trap found was retested with 48 escapes: 16 headings x walk, sprint and sprint-jump, 3 s each.
- Warp radius: a 1 m grid inside 10 m of every warp (about 6,900 starts), each with 16 moves.
- Clip check: temporary MeshColliders on every renderer that has no collider (Play only, removed after), tested against every reached place.
- Nothing in the project was written.
