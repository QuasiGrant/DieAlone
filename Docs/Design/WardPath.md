## 5. Why this answers "worst"
1. Every D came from geometry: a corridor of 3.5 m walls built to stop the mover, and a bowl under the 80 m face (Gate_batch Vesper, Marlow). Four patches tuned rock percentages; none moved the path. This moves it: the cwm and leg 4, the worst frames and the trap, are gone, and a stair takes the step they looped around.
2. Edges are trees and timber, so "all wall" cannot happen outside the 12 s throat, the one closed beat, chosen. Five beats differ by ground, light, sound and what you look at, not only by bearing, and the walk is 61 m shorter.
3. The ledge is graded as two shots at night with the fire present; each beat gets one hero frame Vesper grades before the full capture; Grant walks it gray before dressing.
