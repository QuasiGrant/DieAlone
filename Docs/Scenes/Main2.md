- FIRE: the pack flame material came through the URP upgrade as opaque and drew as a dark blob. Flame particle renderers use Assets/Materials/Flames_Additive.mat (Particles/Unlit, transparent, additive). FirePit.cs flickers its child light (flicker field). FX_Flames_Short scale 0.8, FX_Flames_Tall 0.45, both in the FirePit burningObjects list.
- TOWER (current): deck at 15 m over six flights with cross braces at 4.5 and 9.5 m, room roof at 42 m world. The deck is reachable by the StairRamp check and the Ward is hidden from it by the trees on the slope. Rebuild: Tools/Recipes/build_tower2.cs.
- PACK MATERIALS ON PLAIN GEOMETRY: never put a Revolving Pizza wall or plank material on a primitive cube or ProBuilder shape. Those materials are alpha-clipped atlases; on a box half the surface vanishes, and with clipping off the blank region renders white. Pack materials only on pack meshes. For blockout wood use Assets/Materials/Planks023A_1.0x1.0.mat; for stone Concrete034; for metal PaintedMetal006. Cabin roof is two overhanging plank slabs plus a ridge beam and ProBuilder triangular gables (top vertices collapsed to the ridge line). Camp spacing after the owner review: fire pit 9 m south-east of the cabin door at (251.5, 182.5), generator 4 m behind at (243, 194.5), firewatch tower 13 m north-west at (236, 200); the layout tip positions for those three are superseded.

## Forest and ground cover

- FOREST: terrain trees on Main_TerrainData (7319 instances, 4.2 m jittered grid, kept 3.3 m off trail centres and out of clearings, none west of x 45). Prototypes are prefab variants under Assets/Prefabs/Forest (Tree_Pine1/2/3/5, Tree_Aspen1, Tree_Birch1) that add a CapsuleCollider to the pack prefabs; pines scaled 1.15 to 1.75. Ground cover is terrain detail meshes (Grass1, Grass2, Fern1) in an 11 m band along trails and in clearings. Tree colliders exist only in Play mode; edit-mode raycasts miss them. Setting treePrototypes logs "must use Nature/Soft Occlusion shader" warnings once per prototype; harmless under URP. Rebuild pattern: Tools/Recipes/build_forest.cs.
- FOREST (current): 14.5k trees on a 3 m grid (pines scaled 1.6 to 2.4, kept 2.2 m off trail centres, capped near 1.35 within 45 m of the tower), 5.2k pack bushes as extra tree prototypes without colliders, and 340k ground-cover instances (Grass1/2/3, Fern1/2, Nettle1 as prefab variants Assets/Prefabs/Forest/Detail_* on Assets/Materials/Foliage_Detail.mat with GPU instancing on). Terrain details only draw with TerrainData.SetDetailScatterMode(InstanceCountMode); the default CoverageMode reads layer values as percent cover, so 1 or 2 draws nothing. Detail prototypes also need a material with instancing enabled. Rebuild: Tools/Recipes/build_forest2.cs.
- GROUND COVER (current): twelve detail kinds (Grass1-5, Fern1-3, Nettle1-2, Mushroom1 and 3) with different size ranges and healthy/dry tints, assigned per clump by weight. Rebuild: Tools/Recipes/foliage_variety.cs.
- PERFORMANCE: terrain trees only batch when their materials have GPU instancing on. Pack materials have it off, so the forest was 16k draw calls; tree and bush prototypes now use variants under Assets/Prefabs/Forest (Tree_*, Bush_*, Valley_*) pointing at instanced copies in Assets/Materials/Forest (*_Instanced.mat). Main terrain tree distance 160 m, details 50 m, valley trees 350 m. PC_RPAsset render scale 0.5 (the look filter downsamples to 351 px anyway) and 2 shadow cascades. From the cabin: 333 draw calls, 6.5M triangles. Rebuild: Tools/Recipes/forest_instancing.cs and valley_instancing.cs.

ProBuilder cone mesh is centred on its origin (y -0.5 to 0.5), so a cone of height h sits with its apex at y + h/2.
- VALLEY FOREST: a second terrain "ValleyTerrain" (Assets/Terrain/Valley_TerrainData.asset, 240 x 720 m at position (-240, 0, -160), no collider, allowAutoConnect off) fills the gap between the cliff foot and the ridge with about 3500 terrain trees: pines near the cliff, Aspen/Birch Leafless prototypes toward the fire, tinted dark. Two terrains with different heightmap resolutions log "Stop neighboring" warnings unless allowAutoConnect is false on both.
