Before Grant walks anything, the team must have looked at it as a player: Rook's eye-height contact sheets (Tools/Recipes/main3_review_capture.sh), Marlow's checklist plus his own walk, Vesper against a fixed bar, Pim's task test (5 presses or fewer). Machine checks (sightlines, pushes, rays) are not enough.

Grant WANTS Wren to make judgement calls and proceed without him; he reviews after and is not the gate. Do not answer a quality miss by making him a bottleneck. Checks, revisions and playtest fixes are forward motion: never untick finished work; add new tasks for fixes.

**Why:** 2026-09-30 Grant walked the overnight valley build and called it "untested and unreviewed" (paths like grass, pointless invisible walls, flat lighting, an unfindable day/night toggle, a uniform climb) after it passed about 15 geometry checks. When I then proposed making him approve drawings, stop rulings and untick tasks, he said: "This correction is about quality not that you did it without checking with me" and "I am there to review after, not hand hold."

**How to apply:** Decide, record the call in DECISIONS as Wren's, keep going. Raise the quality bar inside the team. Keep scope to his asks unless the work needs more, and say so. Lighting and look choices: show him pictures, he cannot judge numbers. Related: [[feedback-parallel-work]], [[user-grant]].
