Before Grant walks anything, the team must have looked at it as a player: Rook's eye-height contact sheets (Tools/Recipes/main3_review_capture.sh), Marlow's checklist plus his own walk, Vesper against a fixed bar, Pim's task test (5 presses or fewer). Machine checks (sightlines, pushes, rays) are not enough.

**Why:** 2026-09-30 Grant walked the overnight valley build and called it "untested and unreviewed": paths like grass, pointless invisible walls, flat lighting, an unfindable day/night toggle, a uniform climb. It had passed about 15 geometry checks. I had also ticked 8.9k on my own ruling "pending Grant", built from a revision Marlow never passed, and let a "walkable by tomorrow" target drive a full rebuild.

**How to apply:** Never tick a task that waits on Grant. Never make rulings in his name; list them as open. Hold one unattended task at a time. Treat a date target as secondary to quality and say so. When Grant asks for a fix, keep the scope to his asks. Related: [[feedback-parallel-work]], [[user-grant]].
