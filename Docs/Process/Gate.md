# Gate: before Grant walks a build

DECISIONS 2026-09-30. A done-check that says "passes Gate.md" is passed only when steps 1 to 5 are done. Rook never ticks it; Wren does.
Every reviewer judges against the fixed bar below, never against the last capture. "Fine for gray" is not a pass.

1. **Rook:** WalkChecks.md scripted checks, then `bash Tools/Recipes/main3_review_capture.sh <outDir>`. Hand the sheets path to 2 to 4.
2. **Marlow** (from the sheets, then his own hand walk in the Editor). Each item PASS or FAIL with frame names:
   1. Every trail, both ways, frames every 5 m: path reads apart from ground.
   2. Every invisible stop, from 2 m back: a visible reason, or the wall goes. Exceptions: front exit, Ward path by day.
   3. Every trail end and warp: the path goes on, or ends at a place.
   4. Every place, outside and in: size and purpose read without labels.
   5. Lot and office: the road beyond the gate is in frame.
   6. Each climb leg, first, middle, last frame: legs do not look alike.
   7. Top-down plus four compass views: no empty quarter.
   8. Day and night of the same 10 spots.
   9. F1 in Play: every section reachable by keyboard and by pad.
   10. His hand walk of every new or changed trail.
3. **Vesper:** grades eye-height frames every 25 m against the bar in Docs/Design/Style.md (section 5 and the eye-height bar). Letter grade per zone; D or lower fails. One lighting change per retake, before and after side by side.
4. **Pim:** task test on every menu touched, at Grant's Game view size, pad only and keyboard only, from open. Each task in 5 presses or fewer: switch day/night, warp to the Ward, plus any task Grant named for that menu. Also, from the grayscale sheets: trail mean grey at least 20 above or below the floor beside it (0 to 255, filter on), measured 5 m and 20 m ahead, day and night frames; and every stop sits across the line of travel, covering the full trail width (Wren's call 2026-09-30). Night, on Camp to J and J to Ward only: a practical light in frame 40 grey or more above the frame mean, and the trail edge 20 grey or more from the floor at 5 m inside the lamp; no 20 m test at night, and only where the line can be lost. By day, a leg that fails at 20 m passes by eye if it passes at 5 m, the trail shows to the next bend in every frame, and brush, walls or markers hold the line.
5. **Wren:** all of 2 to 4 PASS, then Grant walks. Any FAIL becomes a new task; nothing is unticked.

Order: 1 first; 2, 3 and 4 run in parallel on the same sheets; Marlow's walk (2.10) needs the Editor, so Wren books it.
