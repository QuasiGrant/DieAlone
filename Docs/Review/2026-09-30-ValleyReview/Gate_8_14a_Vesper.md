## The lip (1.1 m) hides the valley fires

The figures are from Valley.md 5: eye 63.6, path end x -8.5, lip about x -10, valley fires x -110 to -220 with flame tops 20. The fire tops sit 12 to 23 degrees below level. A 1.1 m lip with a top about 1 m deep cuts the view at about 11 degrees down, so every valley fire is hidden. My lip depth is inferred, not measured.

**Do not move the fires west.** Past a 1.1 m lip they only show west of about x -226, which is the foot of the far ridge (x -200 to -240). They would merge into the far front, the burning valley below would be lost, and the fire would read as one line on a hill (forbidden 15).

What I would do instead (these are geometry changes, not lighting, so they do not count against one change per retake):
1. Lower the lip to 0.8 m, the 8.9c height that held the player (0 of 37 past). Give it a top 0.3 to 0.4 m deep that slopes outward, and stand the path end 1.0 m behind its inner face. That puts the eye line about 30 degrees down. The ground past the lip falls about 2 m to the cliff edge (as Main3.md 3.6.5 had it), so the edge does not cut the view. With that, every valley fire top shows, and the bases show from about x -190 west.
2. Raise the valley flame tops from 20 to 35 to 45. That makes the flames 75 to 85 m, 1.5 to 2 times the giants (6.3.2); 60 m is under that bar. With only this change and the lip left at 1.1 m, most fires west of x -140 would still show.
3. Light the floor under the valley fires #6B2A12 so the valley reads as a sea where the bases stay hidden.
