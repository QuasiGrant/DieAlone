4. The player is stopped by rock bands, scree and deadfall at each ridge foot; any invisible stop sits 2 to 4 m behind them (Edges 1.4). Ridge faces within reach are steeper than 45 degrees or lined.

2. 5. **The cleft:** a slot 2.5 m wide, floor 95, walls 106 or higher. Part A runs west 15 m along z 230 to (5, 230). Part B turns right, 90 degrees, and runs north 8 m to the west mouth (4, 238), behind a rock fin 2 m thick on its west side (top 106). Views out of part B run past the fin's north end, ahead and to the left (bearing about 320 to 340, Sable paper), over the ledge. The fin is not extended: those views are set by the fin end and the 2.5 m slot, so a longer fin moves them north with it and the far lip (z 272) gets nearer. **The order as built (rev 6, replaces the paper order):** the fin end (z 237.2) opens onto the ramp and the lip; flame tops first rise over the lip 9 m up the ramp at eye height (5 m at jump); the valley and the full front fill in by the path end.
