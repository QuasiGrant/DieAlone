## Notes
1. **The day.** Tower 60 s. Cheap day (pump, forage B, Camp 3 talk, stove): about 240 s. Full east ring with the store and Camp 1: about 345 s; add the west loop and cave for a completionist: about 660 s. The store against forage is honest: 212 s round trip against 32 s with a 60 percent catch (Dredge). The day's walks are all choices. PASS.
2. **The night.** 234 s of walking every night, with one action at the end. On a cheap day the climb is as long as all the chores. Over a 10 to 16 night run that is 40 to 60 minutes of the same climb. It also breaks the 30 s point-of-interest check (DECISIONS 2026-09-28) for 196 s. Exit 8 repeats one corridor, but the corridor is short and changes; ours is long and never changes.
3. **Run length.** With about three sessions a day (2 to 6 min each) a completionist day runs 25 to 30 min against DailyLoop 4.7's 10 to 15. Cutting the night walk to 150 s saves about 1.5 min a day, 15 to 25 min a run. The rest is sessions; Marlow's simulator and playtest settle it.
4. **What none of the comparables do.** The ordinary world stays in view on every trip to the office, and the losses that happen on it are read from the tower the next dawn. Firewatch's town is scenery; ours is a ledger line.
